# Reflection Report: "First Cloud Journey Meetup"

### Event Information

- **Event Name:** First Cloud Journey Meetup
- **Date and Time:** 09:00 AM, June 13, 2026
- **Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, 02 Hai Trieu Street, Sai Gon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City
- **Role:** Participant

### Purpose of the Event

- Share practical knowledge and experience with AWS services and Cloud system architecture.
- Introduce how Data and technology can be applied to solve real-world business challenges.
- Provide career guidance and equip participants with the skills required to work in technology companies.
- Raise awareness of the importance of corporate culture in today's digital workplace.
- Support university students in building a solid foundation in Cloud Computing and AWS.
- Share practical experience in DevOps, CI/CD, and effective system deployment practices.
- Connect the learning community and encourage knowledge sharing and skill development in the technology field.

### Speakers

- **Hoang Trong** – DevOps Engineer @ Endava Vietnam
- **Dinh Trung Kien** – Lead Developer at Startup
- **Nguyen Minh Tho** – Student
- **Danh Hoang Hieu Nghi** – AI Engineer, AWS Community Builder, AWS Student Builder Group Leader
- **Anh Dat** – Data Analytics Engineer
- **Anh Cuong** – Process Engineer

### Key Highlights

- Learned about the architecture of a URL Shortener system on AWS and how different AWS services work together to provide high availability, scalability, and cost optimization.
- Gained insights into applying Data skills to solve real business problems.
- Learned about recruitment expectations in technology companies, especially the importance of soft skills and cultural fit.
- Explored learning opportunities and support programs for students interested in AWS and Cloud Computing.
- Listened to practical experiences from a DevOps engineer regarding system deployment, CI/CD pipelines, and common operational pitfalls.

### Application to My Project

- Gained a better understanding of Cloud architecture design and applied this knowledge to the FCJ Workshop website project.
- Learned how to select and integrate AWS services based on different system requirements.
- Applied DevOps concepts and CI/CD practices to improve the deployment workflow and project management process.
- Better understood the technical skills required for a future career in Cloud Computing and DevOps.
- Recognized the importance of communication, teamwork, and adapting to corporate culture in a professional environment.

### My Experience at the Event

- The event provided me with valuable practical knowledge about AWS and current technology trends. Besides listening to experienced speakers, I also had the opportunity to interact with students and professionals working in the technology industry. It was a memorable experience that motivated me to continue learning and pursuing a career in Cloud Computing.

#### Networking and Discussion

- Through the event, I had the opportunity to meet and exchange ideas with students who share the same passion for technology, as well as professionals working in the Cloud and DevOps fields. These conversations broadened my perspective, provided valuable learning experiences, and helped me better understand my future career path.

#### Lessons Learned

- Continuous learning and hands-on practice are essential for developing Cloud Computing skills.
- Technical knowledge should be complemented by soft skills and the ability to adapt to corporate culture.
- Building real-world projects is one of the most effective ways to gain practical experience and prepare for future employment.
- Keeping up with the latest developments in AWS, DevOps, and emerging technologies is necessary to remain competitive in the technology industry.
