# Reflection Report: "Agentic AI Build Week"

### Event Information

- **Event Name:** Agentic AI Build Week
- **Date and Time:** 09:00 AM, July 25, 2026
- **Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, 02 Hai Trieu Street, Sai Gon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City
- **Role:** Participant

### Purpose of the Event

- To share the journey and key lessons learned from the Hackathon, while providing participants with opportunities to learn and gain hands-on experience with Agentic AI technologies.
- To promote learning and the adoption of the latest AI technologies, particularly the AI Agent ecosystem (Agentic AI), in addressing real-world problems.
- To provide participating teams with the opportunity to develop their presentation skills and defend their solutions before a panel of expert judges.

### Speakers

- **One Team** : Anh Duy, Tran Dong, Doan Trung, Minh Viet, Anshul Roy
- **Signal Scout** : Hoang Hieu, Quoc Hao, Minh Quan, Cong Minh, Duy Khiem, Tan Luc
- **Plant V** : Hoang Long, Dai Vi, Thuan Phat, Nguyen An, Minh Nghia
- **3KA** : An Khuong, Quoc Huy, Quang Khoi, Thanh Duc, Phuoc Loc, Truong Hung
- **Six Pillars Team** : Hoang Viet, Lam Anh, Van Linh, Canh Nguyen, Minh Nhat, Phuong Huyen

### Key Highlights

- An inspiring keynote delivered by AWS technology leaders on technological transformation, the importance of AI Agents, and the future of automation.
- Product demonstrations and final presentations by the participating teams after 48 hours of continuous development during the Hackathon.
- Technical discussion and Q&A sessions where students and participating teams exchanged ideas and addressed questions regarding solution architecture, operational costs, and real-world implementation challenges.

### Application to My Project

- Automate complex data analysis processes by collecting information automatically and generating comprehensive analytical reports.
- Improve the productivity of professionals, such as Data Analysts, by minimizing repetitive and time-consuming tasks.
- Develop automated feedback systems based on user feedback to continuously improve and optimize business processes.
- Integrate AI Agents into enterprise workflows to enable faster decision-making and reduce human errors.

### My Experience at the Event

- I experienced an energetic atmosphere filled with enthusiasm and a strong passion for learning, shared by a large and vibrant technology community.
- I had the opportunity to listen to the participating teams share their 48-hour journey, from generating their initial ideas to confidently presenting their solutions on stage before a live audience.
- I was able to interact directly with the speakers and participants while listening to insightful technical discussions and practical Q&A sessions that helped answer many real-world questions.
- Within a short period, I gained a substantial amount of new technological knowledge, allowing me to quickly keep up with the latest trends in the IT industry.

#### Networking and Discussion

- Expanded my professional network by connecting with students, software developers, and individuals who share the same passion for AI and software development.
- Had the opportunity to engage in conversations with and learn directly from AWS Solutions Architects, gaining valuable insights from their practical experiences and professional expertise.
- Developed a stronger product mindset and gained a deeper understanding of how to transform a technical idea into a practical business solution.
- Improved my presentation skills, learned how to structure an effective pitch, and developed greater confidence in responding to technical questions under time pressure.

#### Lessons Learned

- There is a significant gap between a prototype and a production-ready solution. Successfully deploying a solution in a real-world environment requires careful consideration of cost, reliability, and governance.
- AI should not be relied upon entirely. Establishing appropriate safety guardrails and maintaining human oversight are essential when integrating AI into enterprise workflows.
- It is important to adopt a proactive mindset, challenge conventional approaches, and continuously embrace new technologies in order to apply them effectively in the workplace.
- Having the courage to think creatively, take initiative, and gain hands-on experience leads to far greater value and personal growth than relying solely on theoretical knowledge.
- Teamwork is essential to the success of any project. It is important to listen to others, express personal ideas, support teammates, share knowledge, and collaborate effectively to achieve common goals.
